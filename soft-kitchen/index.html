3-Day Soft Kitchen

Nine gentle, high-protein meals. Everything soft, everything sauced, one prep session.

The five kitchen rules

  • Everything soft. Vegetables cooked right down, meat sliced thin, nothing raw and nothing with bite left in it.
  • Rice and potato hot and freshly cooked only. Never a leftover, never reheated — so cook only what you will eat.
  • Every plate gets a sauce. Pan gravy, broth or purée. Dry food is the failure mode here.
  • Four hours between meals, and nothing in between. Times are yours to pick; only the gaps matter.
  • Salt properly, and use the ginger. They are doing most of the flavour work.

Prep day — one session

  1. Carrot purée (one batch, three jobs). Boil carrots with ginger until falling apart, blend smooth with garlic oil, a little of their water and salt. It fills all three cold boxes and becomes a dinner sauce.
  2. Roast the three cold-box meats together on one tray at 200 °C — chicken 20 min, turkey 22, pork 30. Rest, then slice all of it thin; thin slices eat far better cold.
  3. Cool everything fully, then fridge it. Boxes are assembled in one minute each morning: purée in, meat on top, herbs over.
  4. Nothing else is made ahead. Mornings and evenings are cooked fresh on the day.

Day 1

Morning · cook fresh · 15 min

Chicken with soft carrots in a ginger gravy

  • Chicken breast (raw, skinless)175 g
  • Carrot2 medium
  • Ginger (fresh)5 g
  • Chives (fresh)5 g
  • Potato starch (for thickening)5 g
  • Olive oil6 g
  1. Carrot in thin coins, into the pan with the oil, a splash of water and salt, lid on 7 min until properly soft.
  2. Push aside. Chicken sliced thin, salted, 5 min.
  3. Grated ginger and a cup of water in, bubble 2 min, then 1 tsp potato starch slurried in cold water — 30 s to gravy.
  4. Chives over. ~15 min.
Midday · from the fridge · 8 min

Cold pork with carrot purée and dill

  • Pork tenderloin (raw, lean)185 g
  • Carrot2 medium
  • Ginger (fresh)4 g
  • Dill (fresh)6 g
  • Garlic-infused olive oil4 g
  1. PREP DAY (shared): same carrot purée batch. Salt the pork loin, roast 30 min, rest 10, then slice it VERY thin — thin cold pork is tender, thick cold pork is work. Cool.
  2. ON THE DAY (1 min): purée into the box, pork on it, dill over.
Evening · cook fresh · 26 min

Cod with rice in a ginger broth

  • Cod fillet (raw)190 g
  • White rice (dry)65 g
  • Carrot2 medium
  • Ginger (fresh)6 g
  • Bay leaf (dried)1 g
  • Parsley (fresh)5 g
  • Olive oil6 g
  1. Rinse the rice, simmer covered in twice its water with salt, 12 min, rest 5 — cook it soft, a little past al dente.
  2. In a wide pan: 2 cups water, sliced ginger, bay leaf, salt, carrot coins — simmer 10 min until the carrots give way completely.
  3. Lay the cod in, lid on, 7 min until it flakes.
  4. Fish out the bay. Rice in the bowl, cod and carrots on top, ladle the broth over. Parsley, oil. Cook only what you will eat — this rice cannot be saved for tomorrow. ~26 min.

Day 2

Morning · cook fresh · 14 min

Cod with soft carrots and dill

  • Cod fillet (raw)215 g
  • Carrot2 medium
  • Ginger (fresh)4 g
  • Dill (fresh)5 g
  • Garlic-infused olive oil6 g
  1. Carrot in thin coins into the pan with the garlic oil, the grated ginger, a splash of water and salt. Lid on, 7 min — cook them right down to soft, no bite left.
  2. Push aside, cod on top, salt it, lid back on, 6 min until it flakes apart.
  3. Dill over, and pour the pan liquid over as the sauce. Everything on the plate is soft. ~14 min.
Midday · from the fridge · 8 min

Cold chicken with carrot purée and dill

  • Chicken breast (raw, skinless)175 g
  • Carrot2 medium
  • Ginger (fresh)4 g
  • Dill (fresh)6 g
  • Garlic-infused olive oil6 g
  1. PREP DAY (shared): the carrot purée is made ONCE for all three boxes — boil carrots and ginger soft, blend with the garlic oil, a little of their water and salt into a smooth spoonable purée. Roast the salted chicken 20 min, rest, slice THIN, cool.
  2. ON THE DAY (1 min): purée into the box, chicken laid on it, dill over. The purée is what keeps it moist — no raw anything, nothing to chew through.
Evening · cook fresh · 26 min

Turkey with rice and a smooth carrot sauce

  • Turkey breast (raw, skinless)140 g
  • White rice (dry)65 g
  • Carrot3 medium
  • Ginger (fresh)5 g
  • Parsley (fresh)5 g
  • Olive oil6 g
  1. Rinse the rice, simmer covered in twice its water with salt, 12 min, rest 5.
  2. Boil the carrot coins with the ginger in just enough salted water to cover, 12 min until falling apart.
  3. Blend them with half their water and half the oil — a smooth sweet sauce, no cream needed. (Same purée as the cold boxes: make extra on prep day and this dinner takes 10 min.)
  4. Salt the turkey, slice thin, fry in the rest of the oil 5 min.
  5. Rice, turkey, sauce poured over, parsley. Cook only what you will eat. ~26 min.

Day 3

Morning · cook fresh · 14 min

Turkey and spinach in a ginger gravy

  • Turkey breast (raw, skinless)155 g
  • Spinach (fresh)4 handfuls
  • Ginger (fresh)5 g
  • Potato starch (for thickening)5 g
  • Olive oil6 g
  1. Slice the turkey THIN across the grain — thin slices stay tender, thick ones go rubbery. Salt, fry in the oil 5 min.
  2. Grated ginger and a cup of water in, bubble 2 min.
  3. 1 tsp potato starch stirred into 2 tbsp cold water, poured in, 30 s to gravy.
  4. Spinach in for 1 min until it collapses soft. Gravy over everything. ~14 min.
Midday · from the fridge · 8 min

Cold turkey with carrot purée and chives

  • Turkey breast (raw, skinless)160 g
  • Carrot2 medium
  • Ginger (fresh)4 g
  • Chives (fresh)6 g
  • Garlic-infused olive oil6 g
  1. PREP DAY (shared): same carrot purée batch as the other boxes. Roast the salted turkey 22 min, rest, slice THIN, cool.
  2. ON THE DAY (1 min): purée into the box, turkey on it, chives snipped over.
Evening · cook fresh · 30 min

Pork with mashed potato and dill gravy

  • Pork tenderloin (raw, lean)160 g
  • Potato (raw)280 g
  • Carrot2 medium
  • Ginger (fresh)5 g
  • Dill (fresh)6 g
  • Potato starch (for thickening)5 g
  • Olive oil4 g
  1. Potato peeled, boiled 18 min with salt. Carrot coins in the same pot for the last 10 min, then mash both together — the carrot goes into the mash, so there is nothing firm on the plate.
  2. Salt the pork, slice into thin medallions, fry 3 min a side, lift out to rest.
  3. Into that pan: grated ginger and a cup of water, scrape the brown bits up, bubble 2 min, then 1 tsp potato starch slurried in cold water — 30 s to gravy. Dill at the end.
  4. Mash loosened with its own cooking water, not butter. Pork on the mash, gravy over the lot. Cook only what you will eat. ~30 min.
